# Jeroen de Jong

Tech lead at [Nedap](https://github.com/nedap). I simplify physical identity & access management at scale: the systems that decide who gets through which door, for hundreds of thousands of people per customer, without anyone noticing they exist.

## Things I care about

- Event-driven services on Postgres and Kafka.
- [Boring tools](https://boringtechnology.club), careful patterns: Postgres for almost everything, outbox tables over dual writes, keyset pagination over offsets.
- Developer experience. Short CI, small PRs, and a release pipeline that ships at any hour of the day.
- Pairing with coding agents (Claude Code, Codex) and writing the rules that keep them honest.
